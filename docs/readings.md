# The readings

[Back to the project README](../README.md)

The first ten are original short summaries, not reproductions of the full articles. Source dates distinguish historical reporting from a current update. Readings 11–21 are original fiction, labeled in the navigation, category, attribution and introductory note; they have no external article link or purported reporting date. Word counts and estimated reading times appear beside the attribution. Longer chapters have paragraph breaks, and short readings use a more compact layout.

1. **Wonder / space:** [NASA’s Webb Takes Star-Filled Portrait of Pillars of Creation](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/nasas-webb-takes-star-filled-portrait-of-pillars-of-creation/), NASA, 19 October 2022.
2. **Unease / climate:** [NOAA confirms 4th global coral bleaching event](https://www.noaa.gov/news-release/noaa-confirms-4th-global-coral-bleaching-event), NOAA, 15 April 2024.
3. **Hope / human ingenuity:** [From Trash To Triumph: The Recycled Orchestra](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/493794763/from-trash-to-triumph-the-recycled-orchestra), Anastasia Tsioulcas, NPR, 14 September 2016, carried by North Country Public Radio.
4. **Carved / subtractive synthesis:** [Geologic Formations](https://www.nps.gov/grca/learn/nature/geologicformations.htm), National Park Service. A saw wave loses upper harmonics as the reading descends through a canyon formed by removal.
5. **Together / additive synthesis:** [Synchronous Fireflies](https://www.nps.gov/grsm/learn/nature/fireflies.htm), National Park Service, updated 14 October 2026. Harmonic components accumulate into one waveform, an analogy for many contributions forming a collective pattern.
6. **Resonance / FM synthesis:** [The Liberty Bell](https://www.nps.gov/inde/learn/historyculture/stories-libertybell.htm), National Park Service. Frequency modulation gives the score a metallic color. It does not reproduce the bell’s actual sound.
7. **A life / domestic violence:** [Court report on Holly Bramley’s murder](https://news.sky.com/story/nicholas-metson-evil-monster-who-cut-his-wifes-body-into-224-pieces-jailed-for-life-13110719), Connor Sephton, Sky News, 8 April 2024. A restrained triangle-wave motif with soft envelopes focuses on the loss of a person. The summary avoids graphic details and does not invent a motive.
8. **Classroom / civilian lives:** [The Minab school becomes a memorial](https://www.ksat.com/news/world/2026/09/06/the-iranian-school-struck-in-the-wars-earliest-attacks-is-now-a-memorial-for-children/), Amir Vahdat, AP, 6 September 2026, carried by KSAT; alongside [AP’s preliminary-investigation reporting](https://wtop.com/world/2026/03/senators-demand-answers-on-school-strike-in-iran-as-growing-evidence-points-to-us-involvement/) first published 11 March 2026, carried by WTOP. The text distinguishes preliminary attribution from a final determination of an accident and dates the September report’s account of unreleased findings. Missing notes and removed additive harmonics interpret absence.
9. **Decision / Brazil:** [TSE announces Lula’s reelection](https://www.tse.jus.br/comunicacao/noticias/2026/Outubro/presidente-do-tse-anuncia-reeleicao-de-lula-e-faz-balanco-do-2o-turno-das-eleicoes-2026), Tribunal Superior Eleitoral, 26 October 2026. The opponent in this result is Flávio Bolsonaro. Harmonic FM and suspended phrases follow the counting process rather than assigning a musical character to a candidate.
10. **Routes / city outskirts:** [Terminal Estação Varginha and its bus connections](https://www.sptrans.com.br/noticias/prefeitura-e-governo-do-estado-inauguram-terminal-estacao-varginha-com-integracao-direta-aos-trilhos-e-reducao-de-tempo-de-viagem/), SPTrans, 13 April 2026. The summary distinguishes a newly created line from routes transferred to the terminal and planned later phases. Interlocking sequences interpret the new connections.
11. **Champions / fiction:** Brazil wins an imagined World Cup final. A bright, brass-like saw-wave lead rises through G-major triads while an answering voice joins the celebration. This is an original composition, not a football anthem or recording of a crowd.
12. **Work / fiction:** AI replaces the paid work of people representing 30% of the global population. The number is invented, with the denominator stated in the story; it is not a forecast or a research finding. A metallic repeating phrase continues while a warmer answering voice loses entries, interpreting the disruption of livelihoods.
13. **One sock / comedy and space — 64 words:** the first Martian laundromat develops a familiar problem. ZZFX supplies an arcade voice with crushed digital edges and small pitch slides.
14. **Two clocks / games and city life — 121 words:** strangers play chess while waiting for a bus. Three-versus-five Euclidean pulses occupy opposite sides of the stereo field; pitch and band-pass shaping distinguish the players.
15. **Before dawn / food and care — 303 words:** an apprentice learns to leave dough alone. Filter envelopes and slight vibrato give individual notes a developing surface; a slow signal varies their placement.
16. **The seam / craft and memory — 618 words:** a projectionist asks for a broken cup to look untouched. An original C4 sample is chopped, reversed and reassembled. Fixed-seed omissions preserve the fragments when reading backward.
17. **Lower rooms / mystery and language — 1,453 words:** a night archivist discovers rooms missing from the plans. Vowel-shaped tones, patterned panning, reverb and echo accompany a substantially longer narrative.
18. **After hours / mallsoft vaporwave — 169 words:** a repair shop outlasts the shopping mall around it. Detuned major-nine pads, slow pitch drift, filtered reflections and a muffled backbeat suggest an abandoned commercial memory.
19. **Room 409 / VHS vaporwave — 301 words:** a home video preserves a holiday hotel. An original electric-key chord plays at half speed, with dark echoes, stereo movement and a sparse beat.
20. **Last delivery / acid electro — 148 words:** a courier follows diversions across a night city. Syncopated kicks, a snare backbeat, a squelching resonant bass and short metallic accents follow the journey.
21. **Below the surface / deep bass:** a night watch follows a small lamp into dark water. D1 (36.7 Hz), A1 and low D minor pentatonic colors form a sustained bass texture. Continuous touch preserves the low register, with restrained harmonics and no percussion.
